# Code of Conduct

This project exists to build shared, high-quality knowledge — that only works if
contributors can disagree on technical substance without it becoming personal.

## Expected Behavior

- Critique ideas and content, not people.
- Assume good faith: most disagreements here are about trade-offs, not right vs. wrong.
- Back technical claims with reasoning or references where possible.
- Be welcoming to newer contributors — everyone's first PR here is a learning curve.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Dismissing contributions based on who made them rather than their content.
- Deliberately submitting low-quality or bad-faith content to disrupt the project.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
official communication channels for the project.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainers (see the repo's main contact listed
in the README). Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and, for repeated or
serious violations, restrict a contributor's participation. Reports will be handled
discreetly and reporters will not be identified without their consent.
